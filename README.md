## Current public projects

- **Portable WIN** — lightweight classroom instruction, WIN grouping, quick checks, proficiency evidence, and reusable teacher-led activities.
- **Praxis 5001 Study Tool** — local-first study engine for Praxis Elementary Education 5001.

### Language Learning

- **Pidgin → ʻŌlelo** — spoken retrieval experiment using familiar Pidgin thoughts as a scaffold into useful ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi.

### Games & Experiments

- **The Islands** — text-first asynchronous strategy experiment with eight rival island kingdoms, hidden simulation, imperfect intelligence, and natural-language orders.

### Writing & Story Projects

- **Peg-Leg Greg Reader** — external illustrated serial reader and production system.
- **Cliff** — fantasy novel reboot.
- **Recovered Story Lab** — older fiction concepts rebuilt into durable project brains and light readers.
