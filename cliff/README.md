# Cliff

Cliff is a fantasy novel project being rebuilt from an older, sprawling draft into a new, disciplined Book 1.

This is a **resurrection, not a continuation**. The historical draft contains characters, scenes, world ideas, and one especially strong central plot worth recovering, but its prose and continuity do not automatically control the reboot.

## Working direction

Cliff survived roughly ten formative years on a monster-infested island by becoming unusually good at defensive magic, especially barriers, and by learning when running was smarter than fighting. He enters magical society wanting safety, friends, food, comfort, and something resembling an ordinary life.

The likely Book 1 center is the Dollmaker: a brilliant, isolated enchanter whom Cliff treats with compassion when others keep their distance. Cliff's insight into life, imperfection, and creation helps the Dollmaker discover a horrifying path toward making truly living artificial people through human souls.

The story is therefore not mainly about whether Cliff can survive a magical academy. It is about a man whose defining gift is making people safe learning that physical protection is not enough when his trust and judgment can help create the danger.
