# Triggerman — STORY SEED

## Core Hook

The power is not interesting because it wins fights. It corrupts the evidence people normally use to know whether another person chose them. The protagonist must live inside a relationship whose apparent intimacy may be inseparable from coercion.

## Story Engine

The story treats manufactured affection as the central violation rather than a romantic shortcut. A half-remembered backlash or reciprocal cost may constrain the power, but its exact rule remains unsettled until recovered or deliberately rebuilt.

## Rebuild Edge

Reconstruct the power limits, backlash, relationship chronology, and later crime/bank sequence without normalizing the coercion. The story should ask what can still be real after choice has been tampered with.
