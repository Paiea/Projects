# Tower of Aescalon — STORY SEED

## Core Hook

Humanity is climbing a dangerous tower. A lower-floor academy does more than teach: it tries to assemble combinations of people who may survive as long-term climbing teams. In that environment, reliable water creation stops looking useless.

## Story Engine

The power should become valuable because the environment makes water, carrying capacity, expeditions, recovery, and logistics matter. It should not secretly transform into an unrelated god-tier attack ability. The academy-team system gives friendships, rivalries, and role selection mechanical consequences.

## Rebuild Edge

Recover or rebuild the tower rules, party members, academy politics, and first meaningful expedition. Keep the central inversion intact: ordinary utility becomes extraordinary when the world takes ordinary needs seriously.
