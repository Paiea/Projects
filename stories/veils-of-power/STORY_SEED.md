# Veils of Power — STORY SEED

## Core Hook

Relatives and academy peers resent the protagonist's status because his visible magic seems unimpressive. Respected insiders treat him very differently. The hidden explanation is not theatrical weakness: his strength is already sustaining a protection he cannot casually abandon.

## Story Engine

Visible weakness and actual burden must coexist. Every temptation to demonstrate power creates a real cost because using more magic may require weakening what he protects. The social mystery works best when informed people reveal the mismatch through behavior before exposition explains it.

## Rebuild Edge

Rebuild what the barriers protect, why secrecy exists, what the family expects of its head, and what would force him to choose between public action and continuous protection.
