# Umbral Healer's Shade — STORY SEED

## Core Hook

He has built an identity around helping people and interprets his own worsening condition as a curse or terminal illness. His journey eventually leads to a witch who may understand that his gift was never healing magic in the first place.

## Story Engine

The stable distinction is pain relief versus healing. His magic takes pain; it does not repair the wound. He does not begin as a knowing martyr. The story starts because he thinks he is sick or dying and does not understand why.

## Rebuild Edge

The exact mechanism of his sickness, what happens to patients after pain removal, the witch's role, and the ending remain unsettled. Do not harden those details without recovered source or a deliberate new author decision.
