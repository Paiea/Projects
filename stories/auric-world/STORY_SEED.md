# Auric World — STORY SEED

## Core Hook

Gold is mana, and auric beings are imagined as gold-based life. Angels consume or refine auric matter and wield controlled halos as weapons. Humans inherit distorted religious stories from ancient conflicts whose physical remains may still sit beneath ordinary geology.

## Story Engine

Gold/mana should behave like conserved matter rather than generic spell points. Halos are functional weapons and stored power, not decoration. The old moral categories of angel and demon are politically unreliable. One material rule should generate economic, ecological, military, religious, and archaeological consequences.

## Related Possibility: Seven Lamps

A recovered architectural-magic framework used the Seven Lamps as principles for building, understanding, and adjusting magic. It overlaps with later Auric World exploration, but the relationship is not settled. Preserve it as a possibility until a rebuild shows whether it belongs inside this world or deserves its own project.

## Rebuild Edge

Find the intimate protagonist-scale story that reveals the enormous material system gradually. Do not begin with an encyclopedia of angel/demon history.

## Status

**ACCEPTED SEED DIRECTION.** Conservation, auric life, controlled halos, and unreliable religious history are accepted rebuild material. The Seven Lamps relationship remains a possibility.
