# The Dragon Spotter — STORY SEED

## Core Hook

The profession contains decaying procedures, ignored expertise, false alarms, bureaucracy, and institutional memory before the first dragon appears. When the impossible event happens, the person whose credibility is compromised may also be the person who knows what everyone else forgot.

## Story Engine

The protagonist does not need to become a dragon slayer. Recognition, warning, interpretation, procedure, and the guilt of neglected preparedness are enough to make the profession consequential. The returning threat should activate both expertise and credibility failure.

## Rebuild Edge

Rebuild the spotting institution, what work was falsified, what old procedures survive, and the first return event. Preserve the mundane profession as the story engine.
