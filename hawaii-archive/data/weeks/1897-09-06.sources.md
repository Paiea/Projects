# September 6–12, 1897 — Source Ledger

This ledger supports the bounded expansion of the Hawaiʻi Archive Revival proving week. It is research/navigation evidence, not a replacement for the item-level `source_url` and `source_label` fields.

## Governing rule

Follow historical attention. Do not manufacture balance. Preserve repetition when the repetition itself shows what people cared about. Preserve multiple voices and places. Add ordinary life wherever the archive gives it to us. Preserve the lag between something happening and people learning about it.

### Ke Aloha Aina

Archival authority candidate: Hawaiʻi State Archives Digital Archives, *Ke Aloha Aina, 1897–1898*, reference MFL1-A2.

- https://digitalarchives.hawaii.gov/item/ark:70111/j5z

The September 11, 1897 issue is already represented through sourced/transcribed speech material in the feed. Direct issue extraction remains the preferred next step for widening beyond the famous speeches into whatever else the paper itself prioritized.

The September 6 Palace Square speeches are event-time records. The *Ke Aloha Aina* account was published September 11. Preserve that five-day event-to-publication lag instead of presenting the speech and newspaper appearance as simultaneous.

## Extraction order

1. Preserve and annotate the existing September 6 speech records with event/publication lag. Completed for the current speech set.
2. Expand directly dated petition district records across five islands. Current checkpoint: 34 district pages and 47 total feed records.
3. Mine the September 11 *Ke Aloha Aina* issue directly, following what the issue emphasizes rather than a topic quota.
4. Mine the September 10 *Ka Nupepa Kuokoa* issue directly, including ordinary notices or other material only where the issue actually gives it attention.
5. Expand to another newspaper published during September 6–12 only after the two known issue lanes are exhausted enough to judge what the week itself was talking about.

## Evidence discipline

- A district petition page can support a collective organizing/protest record. It does not support invented dialogue or a claim about the exact moment every person signed.
- Multiple sheets from one district are not automatically multiple feed posts. Preserve another sheet only when it adds a materially distinct place, participant structure, or other historical signal.
- A newspaper publication date is not automatically the event date.
- A later scholarly transcription can support interpretation while remaining downstream of the historical newspaper.
- Failed machine retrieval is not evidence. Do not fill inaccessible issue interiors from expectation, memory, or neighboring issues.
