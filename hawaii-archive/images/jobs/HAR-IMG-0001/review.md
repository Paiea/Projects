# HAR-IMG-0001 — Review

## Source authority

Library of Congress record: `https://www.loc.gov/item/89709348/`

The source group photograph is dated circa 1893 and identifies J.K. Kaulia among the delegates. The feed uses a public-domain crop of Kaulia from that archival photograph. The image predates the September 1897 Palace Square speech and is a **near** relationship: same person, earlier portrait.

## Restoration review

Method: `deterministic-tonal`

Approved class: `restore-neutral-albumen`

The v1 restoration is intentionally conservative: neutralize some albumen warmth, recover contrast, slightly lift faded midtones, and add restrained sharpness in-browser. It does **not** claim scratch removal, facial reconstruction, invented hair detail, altered clothing seams, or changed composition.

Identity lock: approved. Geometry lock: approved. Object/content lock: approved because the renderer changes tonal presentation only.

## Color decision

**approved — plausible best estimate**

Method: `best-estimate-hand-tint`

The proof-of-concept palette uses warm brown skin, white shirt/cuffs, a dark charcoal suit, muted brown-gray waistcoat, and a neutral warm studio surround. Those choices are period-plausible and consistent with the tonal structure of the source, but the albumen print does not preserve the actual hues.

The public view must label the color state as a reconstruction/estimate and keep Original + Restored B&W available.

## Review result

APPROVED for Image OS v1 and feed use as a near-match portrait. Do not describe it as a photograph from the September 6, 1897 meeting.
