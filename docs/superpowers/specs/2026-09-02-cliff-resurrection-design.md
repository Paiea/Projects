# Cliff Resurrection Design

## Goal

Resurrect the old Cliff fantasy project as a disciplined new writing project inside `Paiea/Projects` without treating the 733-page historical draft as current canon.

The reboot should preserve the strongest character/world/story material, discard accumulated bloat, and establish one clean Book 1 authority that future chats and workers can continue from GitHub.

## Project Placement

Cliff will be an **internally hosted Writing & Story Project** inside `Paiea/Projects`.

Initial project path:

- `cliff/`

It does **not** need a separate repository yet. Peg-Leg Greg has earned a dedicated repository through scale and production complexity. Cliff has not.

No project-local `AGENTS.md` will be added initially. Root routing plus a compact `PROJECT_STATE.md` is sufficient.

## Authority Model

The old uploaded PDF and any historical prose extracted from it are **RECOVERED SOURCE, NOT CANON**.

Authority order for the reboot:

1. accepted new manuscript prose on `main` in `cliff/manuscript/BOOK_01.md`
2. accepted durable direction in `cliff/PROJECT_STATE.md`
3. recovered old-draft material recorded in `cliff/recovery/OLD_DRAFT_RECOVERY.md`
4. old chats, generated variants, notes, and the original PDF

Historical material may inspire or supply facts for reconsideration, but nothing in it automatically binds the reboot.

New story choices that materially establish canon should be deliberate rather than silently promoted from recovered notes.

## Minimum Project Structure

### `cliff/README.md`

Human-facing project page.

It should explain:

- what Cliff is
- that this is a resurrection/reboot of older writing
- where current manuscript authority lives
- that recovered material is non-canon source material
- current project status

### `cliff/PROJECT_STATE.md`

Compact hot state for fresh-worker re-entry.

It should own:

- purpose
- exact authority paths
- current Book 1 premise and thematic spine
- accepted salvage/rebuild/discard decisions
- live open questions only
- current `NEXT_TASK`
- compact `RE-PROMPT`

It should not become a giant lore bible or historical dump.

### `cliff/recovery/OLD_DRAFT_RECOVERY.md`

Cold, retrievable archaeology distilled from the old draft.

Its job is to record useful fossils, grouped by epistemic status, without making them canon.

It should preserve the strongest recovered material such as:

- Cliff surviving roughly ten formative years on monster-infested Norend Isle
- barrier/projected-aura specialization born from survival
- his tendency to run rather than fight when survival demands it
- improvised creature names such as calling a terrifying predator `Ralph`
- Loben as a major island companion with a later monster/shape-shifter reveal in old material
- Thalia as an island companion / former romantic relationship in old material
- Elena, Lily, Fortune Legion, magical crafting/economics, and the academy as salvage candidates
- the Dollmaker/Sam material as the strongest recovered central plot
- the old magical-underworld family material as potentially useful later-book material

The file should also explicitly quarantine discarded or dangerous drift:

- sprawling six-book immortality escalation
- generic ancient-evil/prophecy expansion
- allurement used as sexual compulsion or harem machinery
- incest-adjacent sexual branches
- automatic preservation of old continuity merely because it exists

### `cliff/manuscript/BOOK_01.md`

The single new manuscript authority for Book 1.

It begins empty except for a short authority note until replacement prose is deliberately drafted and accepted.

The reboot should not paste old chapters into this file and then edit around them. New prose starts from page one.

## Book 1 Direction

### Working premise

After surviving ten years on an island of monsters by mastering defensive magic, Cliff enters magical society wanting little more than safety, friends, food, and an ordinary life. His unusual barrier talent eventually brings him into the orbit of a brilliant, lonely, controversial enchanter known as the Dollmaker. Cliff treats him with compassion when others keep their distance, and one piece of well-meant advice helps the Dollmaker discover how to make truly living dolls by using human souls.

### Thematic spine

Cliff survived by putting barriers between himself and danger. Book 1 forces him to learn that protecting people physically is not enough when his judgment, trust, and compassion can help create the danger.

Cliff's empathy should remain a real virtue. The Dollmaker tragedy works because Cliff is not foolish to see the man's loneliness or humanity. He is wrong only if he assumes understanding someone makes them safe.

### Cliff's durable protagonist core

Keep:

- island-raised survival competence
- defensive/barrier magic as his defining specialization
- practical instinct to flee unwinnable danger
- odd island references and improvised monster names
- culture shock around ordinary civilization
- desire for comfort and normality rather than glory
- capacity for warmth, loyalty, and empathy

His magic should thematically express safety. Existing recovered material where his barriers feel warm, cocoon-like, or deeply secure is a useful direction to reinterpret rather than copy automatically.

### Academy function

The academy should be a pressure cooker and social transition environment, not thirty chapters of class-tour exposition.

Use classes, factions, dorm life, work, food, money, and mundane routines when they create character pressure, relationships, humor, consequence, or setup. Do not preserve scenes merely because they show normal academy life.

The modern craft target is to make mundane scenes carry story rather than stopping story for slice-of-life filler.

### Fortune Legion

Keep Fortune as a salvage candidate, but sharpen its function.

Preferred direction: practical opportunity, resources, trade, crafting, fieldwork, unconventional problem solving, and people who profit from finding value others miss. Avoid vague `luck` identity unless it becomes concrete in the world.

### Supporting cast

Keep as salvage candidates, not locked old-continuity canon:

- **Loben:** island companion whose old reveal as something non-human is worth retaining or reinterpreting.
- **Lily:** goofy, grounded friend energy with potential scout/observer usefulness.
- **Elena:** more experienced enchanter/mentor figure who should have independent wants and friction rather than simply admiring Cliff.
- **Thalia:** important island-history figure whose exact reboot role remains open.

Other old characters such as Nelly, Gregor, Judge, Ethan, and the family underworld cast may be recovered later only if Book 1 needs them.

## Dollmaker Arc

The Dollmaker is the primary reason to resurrect this project and should be treated as the likely Book 1 spine rather than one subplot among many.

Core recovered mechanism worth preserving:

1. Cliff's unusual defensive magic leads him toward enchantment/crafting questions and eventually the Dollmaker.
2. The Dollmaker is brilliant, controversial, isolated, and genuinely lonely.
3. Other people warn Cliff away.
4. Cliff befriends him because he sees a person rather than only the reputation.
5. The Dollmaker cannot make his lifelike creations truly alive.
6. Cliff offers a compassionate insight centered on imperfection and real life.
7. The Dollmaker interprets that insight in a horrifying technical direction and begins using human souls.
8. People die because of the experiments.
9. The Dollmaker sincerely thanks Cliff for the idea that unlocked his breakthrough.
10. Authorities appear to stop him.
11. Cliff later recognizes the Dollmaker surviving in a perfected artificial body and disappearing.
12. Cliff must carry the unresolved moral weight of having helped through kindness rather than malice.

The exact wording, mechanics, victim scale, investigation structure, and final confrontation remain open for redesign.

## Delayed Material

The magical-underworld family, powerful grandfather, House of Goat, shadow legacy, Ethan/Lily political pressure, and related crime-family material should not automatically compete with the Dollmaker for Book 1's center.

Default: seed lightly or delay to a later book.

Promote any of it into Book 1 only if it directly strengthens Cliff's immediate arc rather than expanding the series map.

## Explicit Non-Goals

Do not:

- continue from old Chapter 31 or any other old endpoint
- preserve old prose for sentimental reasons
- build a six-book master plan before Book 1 works
- add specialist lore files before recurring complexity earns them
- turn every recovered idea into canon
- use prophecy/ancient-evil escalation as a substitute for character consequence
- use magical attraction as coercive sexual wish fulfillment
- recreate generic academy-fantasy pacing simply because the setting is an academy

## Initial Success Criteria

The bootstrap is successful when:

1. Cliff is registered as an internal Writing & Story Project.
2. `cliff/README.md`, `cliff/PROJECT_STATE.md`, `cliff/recovery/OLD_DRAFT_RECOVERY.md`, and `cliff/manuscript/BOOK_01.md` exist.
3. The old draft is explicitly subordinate recovered material.
4. Book 1 has one clear premise, thematic spine, and likely central Dollmaker arc.
5. Future workers can restart from GitHub without needing this chat.
6. The next executable edge is replacement opening work rather than more infrastructure.

## First Execution Edge

After the project skeleton is accepted and merged, the first creative task should be to design and draft a replacement opening sequence from page one.

That opening should establish Cliff's specific appeal quickly: survival competence, defensive magic, island-shaped worldview, odd naming/humor, and his hunger for ordinary civilized safety.

It should not begin by reproducing the old chapter structure merely because that is how the historical draft opened.
